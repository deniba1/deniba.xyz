Changelog

What has changed, newest first. The scoring rules are public, so when they change it is written down here with the date. Anything that alters how an account is scored is marked scoring.

25 October 2026 Twelve more scoring factors, a new look, and guides

  • Scoring. Twelve new factors. Toward main: owning a group with 100 or 1,000 members, badges from ten or more different games, a first badge earned over a year ago, a created place with 100,000 or more visits, and wearables first picked up six months or two years ago. Toward alt: dozens of badges from one or two games, a username with the shape of a machine-made name, a display name with an alt-style word, a description that mentions a ban, a test account, or buying and selling accounts, hundreds of followers on an account that has never played, and following hundreds of accounts with no followers. A username ending in a year now counts less than a run of digits. Accounts last online over a year ago are flagged for information only.
  • Results. Stats are grouped into Social, Activity, Inventory and avatar, and Identity, each figure coloured by which way it pushes the score. Detail text is no longer cut off. Account age has its own tile, and the status tile shows when the account was last online. A balance bar shows how many points pushed each way, the score ring animates in, and every result has a copy-link button.
  • Design. New type (Inter and Sora), a sticky header, a proper front page with example lookups, softer cards, a highlighted Pro plan, and a retuned light theme. Press / anywhere to jump to the search box.
  • Guides. Eight guides on alt accounts, from what they are and how to spot one by hand to badge walks, limiteds, moderation and account safety. Long guides get an "On this page" list. New About, Contact and Changelog pages.
  • Fixes. The badge check failed when the server's Roblox login had a stray character in it; the value is now cleaned before use, and a rejected login reports itself clearly instead of as a generic error.

1 October 2026 Deep check and badge timing

  • Deep check. A second pass that reads the account's friends list, estimates each friend's age from their user ID, looks closely at a spread of them, and checks which of them are friends with each other. The result is drawn as a web and moves the score. Free accounts get one a day, Pro ten.
  • Scoring. Badge timing. Ten or more badges within ten minutes count as a burst, the pattern of a badge-walk game. Burst badges no longer count as play, and an account whose badges are mostly bursts is flagged. Badges earned on fifteen or more different days count toward main.
  • Admin accounts with no limits and a debug view of each Roblox call, for running the service.

30 September 2026 Accounts, Pro and policies

  • Free accounts and a Pro subscription, with daily allowances per plan. Sign in with email or Google.
  • Terms of Service, Privacy Policy and Refund Policy. Ads on the free tiers.
  • Light and dark themes, following the system setting unless you choose.
  • Rate limits per account, per network and site-wide, so that one heavy user cannot exhaust Roblox's allowance for everyone.
  • Search metadata, a sitemap and icons.

27 September 2026 First release

  • The checker: enter a username or user ID, get a 0 to 100 alt score built from public profile data, with every signal and its points listed. Account age, friends, followers, groups, Roblox badges, game badges, favorites, inventory and limiteds, avatar, created places, username and description patterns.
  • Runs as a Supabase Edge Function, or self-hosted with the included Python server. The source is public.
