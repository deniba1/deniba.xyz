Contact

Roblox Alt Checker is run by one person, so there is one address for everything. Email deniba1@proton.me and you will get a reply from a human, usually within three working days.

What to include

Something is broken
Say which page you were on, what you typed, what you expected, and what you saw instead. If an error message appeared, copy it word for word. The username or user ID you were checking helps a lot: most failures come from a particular Roblox profile that behaves differently from the rest.
Billing or a refund
Write from the email address on your account and say which payment it is about. The refund policy explains what is refunded and when; your first payment is refundable for 14 days without any reason being needed. Please write before disputing a charge with your bank, because that is almost always slower for you.
Your data
You can delete your account yourself from the account page, which removes it immediately. For anything else covered by the privacy policy, such as a copy of what is held about you, send the request from the address on your account.
A concern about how your Roblox account appears
The checker shows only what Roblox already displays publicly on your profile, and keeps nothing about it beyond a ten-minute cache. If you believe the score misrepresents your account, send the username and a line about what looks wrong. The scoring rules are public and apply equally to every account, so individual scores are not edited by hand, but reports of a signal that misfires are how the rules get better.
A mistake in a guide
Roblox changes how badges, limiteds and moderation tools work, and the guides try to keep up. Point to the page and the sentence, and say what has changed. Corrections are noted on the page with a date.
Press, research or partnerships
Say who you are and what you are working on. Requests for bulk data about Roblox users will be declined, because the site deliberately does not keep any.

What not to send

Never send your Roblox password or your .ROBLOSECURITY cookie to this site or to anyone else, for any reason. Nobody here will ever ask for them. If someone claiming to be from Roblox Alt Checker does, it is a scam; the guide on account safety explains how these work and what to do.

Reports about other people

This site cannot act on another player's Roblox account, and does not keep any record of who was looked up. If someone is breaking Roblox's rules, report them through Roblox's own Report Abuse button on their profile or inside the game, which goes to Roblox's moderation team. If someone is using this site to harass you, send what you have and it will be looked at.

Postal address

DENIXYZ is a sole proprietorship based in California, USA. A postal address is available on request for legal correspondence; email is faster for everything else.
