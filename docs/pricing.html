Questions

What is a deep check?
A normal lookup scores the account's own profile. A deep check also reads its friends list and looks at a spread of the friends on it: how old their accounts are, whether their profiles have anything on them, and whether they are friends with each other. It draws the result as a web and adjusts the score. It needs an account, and a deep check of an account with no visible friends isn't counted.
What counts as a lookup?
Each account you check that isn't already in the short-term cache. Opening the same result again within a few minutes is free, and a lookup that fails isn't counted (up to ten a day). Allowances reset at 00:00 UTC.
Can a free allowance run out early?
Occasionally. To keep the service fair, free and guest lookups also share a daily cap per network (a household, school or mobile carrier) and across the whole site. If one of those is reached, free lookups pause until 00:00 UTC. Pro accounts aren't affected by the shared caps.
How do I cancel?
From your account page, open “Manage billing” and cancel there. You keep Pro until the end of the period you've paid for, then drop back to Free.
Can I get my money back?
Yes. Your first payment is refundable for 14 days, no questions asked. The details are in the Refund Policy.
Does Pro make the score more accurate?
No. Every plan gets the same checks and the same score. It's an estimate from public data on any plan, never proof.
Which payment methods can I use?
Checkout is run by Stripe and offers the cards and wallets available in your country. We never see or store your card details.
